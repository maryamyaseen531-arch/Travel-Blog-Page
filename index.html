BEFORE YOU GO

Little Tips, Better Trips

A few simple ideas to help you enjoy your visit.

What is the best time to visit Karachi?

November to February is usually more comfortable for outdoor activities because the weather is cooler.

What should I take with me?

Carry water, sunscreen, comfortable shoes and a charged phone. Keep your belongings safe while travelling.

How can I travel around the city?

Plan your route before leaving and choose reliable transport. Check local conditions and traffic.
